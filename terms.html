What Venue Portal Is

Venue Portal is a booking infrastructure platform that connects independent venue owners with event promoters. We provide the tools — inquiry intake, booking management, payment processing via Stripe Connect — and charge a flat platform fee per confirmed booking.

Venue Portal is a platform, not a party to venue agreements. Any contract for use of a physical space is between the venue owner and the promoter. We facilitate the transaction — we do not own, operate, or manage any venue listed on the platform.

03

Booking Process

The booking flow works as follows:

  • A promoter submits an inquiry through the public venue listing page
  • The venue owner reviews the inquiry and chooses to Accept or Decline
  • On Accept, a Stripe Checkout payment link is automatically generated and emailed to the promoter
  • The booking is confirmed when the promoter completes payment
  • The venue owner receives their payout via their connected Stripe account after fees are deducted

Accepted inquiries are not confirmed bookings until payment is received. Venue owners should not consider a date reserved until the deposit clears and the booking status shows "Confirmed."

Cancellations & Refunds

Cancellation and refund policies are set between the venue owner and the promoter. Venue Portal does not issue refunds directly and is not liable for disputes between parties regarding use of a physical venue.

If a venue owner cancels a confirmed booking, they are responsible for coordinating any refund with the promoter through Stripe. The $50 platform fee is non-refundable in all cases, including cancellations initiated by the venue.

If a promoter fails to complete payment after an inquiry is accepted, the booking will remain in "Pending" status and the date will not be blocked on the venue's calendar. No money is owed in that scenario.
